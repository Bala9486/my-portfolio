CAN-Based Automotive Dashboard (3-ECU Network)

Three PIC18F4580 ECU nodes exchanging live vehicle data over a CAN bus and showing it on a dashboard display.

  • ECU1 reads speed (ADC) and gear (matrix keypad); ECU2 reads RPM and indicator input; ECU3 is the dashboard node.
